Assistance & FAQs

Android Sideloading FAQ & Troubleshooting

Play Protect warning on Android device expand_more
Google Play Protect regularly warns users when installing an APK downloaded through a web browser if the developer certificate hasn't paid Google's annual developer registry. Tap 'More details' and then tap 'Install anyway'. You can inspect the complete source on GitHub.
Why does ContinueO require Unknown Apps permission? expand_more
Android requires explicit user approval before any browser or file manager can trigger package installation. This is a built-in Android OS security barrier ensuring no background application can install software without your direct permission.
Is student data shared with external servers? expand_more
No. ContinueO operates completely client-side. The app has no backend servers, no analytics databases, and no telemetry trackers. All portal interactions happen exclusively between your smartphone's WebView and BMSIT's official Contineo portal endpoints.
Updating the APK without losing saved accounts expand_more
When installing an update (e.g. from v1.2.7 to v1.2.8), simply download and install the new APK over the existing installation without uninstalling. Android will perform an in-place upgrade, preserving your encrypted keystore and saved student profile accounts.
