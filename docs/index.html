Setup in 3 Steps

Follow these quick guides to get running in under a minute.

Android App Setup

  1. 1
    Download the APK: Tap the Download APK button to save ContinueO-AutoLogin.apk on your phone.
  2. 2
    Allow Installation: Open the downloaded file. If Google Play Protect shows a prompt (*"Harmful app blocked"*), tap "Install anyway" (standard for self-signed APKs).
  3. 3
    Save Profile & Tap Login: Enter your USN, DOB, and ID Card number on first launch. Tap LOGIN NOW 🚀!

Desktop Extension Setup

  1. 1
    Download & Extract ZIP: Download the extension release ZIP from GitHub and extract it to a folder.
  2. 2
    Open Extensions Page: In Chrome, Brave, or Edge, visit chrome://extensions and toggle Developer mode in the top right.
  3. 3
    Load Unpacked: Click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted folder. Pin ContinueO to your toolbar!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might wonder about security, installation, and updates.

Why does Google Play Protect say "Harmful app blocked"?
Because ContinueO is an open-source, client-side app distributed independently outside the Google Play Store, Google Play Protect flags unrecognized self-signed certificates. To proceed, simply tap "Install anyway" (located directly above the blue "Got it" button). The app requires only standard internet access and runs zero background tracking.
Are my college credentials safe?
Yes, 100%. Your USN, Date of Birth, and ID Card number never leave your device. On Android, they are stored inside Google's EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by an Android Keystore MasterKey (AES-256-GCM). In the browser extension, credentials stay in sandboxed local storage protected by the Web Crypto API.
Can I download subject notes ([NOTES]) and circulars in the app?
Yes! Tapping [NOTES] or any PDF/circular link routes the file directly to your phone's Downloads directory via Android's native DownloadManager. Session cookies and referer headers are injected automatically so authenticated downloads succeed without permission errors.
How does Over-The-Air (OTA) updating work?
ContinueO includes a multi-tier update checker (Fastly CDN, release redirect, and GitHub API) that detects new versions without rate limits. Tapping "Update (OTA)" downloads the APK and prompts package installation directly over Wi-Fi/cellular. All builds share a permanent release keystore so updates install seamlessly without signature conflicts.
What happens if the portal shows a CAPTCHA or OTP?
The app and extension strictly follow safety best practices. If a CAPTCHA or OTP challenge appears, automation pauses and enables a "Continue Manually" mode so you can solve it yourself in the WebView or browser tab.
